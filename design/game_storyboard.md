## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

 A mysterious adventurous action game called "The Hangover in my House" Where the player moves from room to room to find useful items to help him survive without being scene or waking up the villain who would kill him if discovered.

**Storyline:**

The player is trapped inside his own house and must find items for survival like water, a knife for protection, food, and bedding to sleep on and run back to the attic to store it and stay safe. Someone has entered the home drunk and wants to sleep off his hangover but to awaken can be dangerous and start a fight, not realizing he's in the wrong house. The ultimate goal is to leave either the front or back door or a window, If he is able to find his charger for his cell phone he may be able to call 911 and get some help.
